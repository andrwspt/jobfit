# JobFit Distribution Drafts

## Reddit — r/jobs (or r/careerguidance)

**Title:** I built a free offline tool that shows you exactly what your resume is missing vs. the job description

**Body:**
Recruiters say they scan a resume for ~7 seconds. I wanted to know what they're looking for in those 7 seconds, so I built JobFit.

It's a free, offline, single-file tool: you paste your resume and a job description, and it shows you your match score + the exact keywords you're missing.

No server. No account. No tracking. Your data never leaves your browser.

Live here: https://github.com/andrwspt/jobfit

If it helps you land an interview, a $1 tip (Ko-fi/PayPal in the repo) keeps the tools coming.
