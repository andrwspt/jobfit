# JobFit

Free, offline resume gap checker. Paste your resume and a job description, get your match score plus the exact keywords you're missing.

**No server. No account. No tracking.**

**👉 Live demo:** https://andrwspt.github.io/jobfit/

---

## Why it exists

Recruiters spend ~7 seconds scanning a resume. JobFit reverse-engineers the job description to show you what you're missing — so you can add it before they do.

## How to use

1. Open `index.html` (double-click — no install).
2. Paste your resume.
3. Paste the job description.
4. Click **Check fit** → get a match score and a list of missing keywords.
5. Add the missing keywords to your resume → apply with confidence.

## Built for

- Job seekers tailoring their resume per application
- Career coaches helping clients match job descriptions
- Anyone who wants to know *why* they didn't get the interview
